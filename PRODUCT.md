# DiscreteLab

A client-side academic laboratory for students demonstrating Module 4: lattices and Boolean algebra. Its five labs derive visible graphs, schedules, circuits and explanations from editable user inputs. All existing mathematical rules and tests remain authoritative. No authentication, backend or external data service is needed.

The current redesign adds an overview route, dark/light themes, routing, export/share, scheduling timelines, K-maps and keyboard access while preserving the working demonstration workflows.

Current presentation: the warm Mathematical Atelier brief supersedes the Observatory styling. Preserve all routes and engines while using warm dark/parchment light surfaces, editorial headings, explicit K-map application and structured reports.

The overview follows the approved Proof Atlas art direction: a live mathematical plate and an illustrated index of the same five applications. Product captures are illustrative sample views; named links lead to the editable laboratories. Motion is an optional explanatory layer with static, reduced-motion and mobile alternatives.
